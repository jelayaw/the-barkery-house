OUR STORY

Made With Love.
Baked For Best Friends.

At The Barkery House, we believe dogs deserve treats made with the same care and love they give us every day. That's why our treats are made fresh with simple ingredients and plenty of tail-wagging approval.

WHY WE STARTED

Because They’re Family, Too.

The Barkery House started with one simple idea: our dogs deserve better than boring treats made without much thought.

We wanted to create something that felt a little more special — treats made fresh, with simple ingredients and the kind of care we would expect for our own four-legged family members.

Whether it’s a reward for being a very good dog, a birthday surprise, or just because those puppy eyes are impossible to resist, every treat from The Barkery House is made to turn an ordinary moment into a tail-wagging one.
