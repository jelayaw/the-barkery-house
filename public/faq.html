FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?
We’ve Got Treats... And Answers.

Find answers to common questions about our treats, orders, shipping, ingredients, and everything in between.

What ingredients are used in your dog treats?

Our treats are made with simple, thoughtfully selected ingredients. Ingredient details are listed on each product page so you can review them before placing an order.

Are your treats made fresh?

Yes. Our treats are prepared fresh so your pup receives something made with care instead of something that has been sitting around for ages.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! Orders with a subtotal of $45 or more qualify for free shipping. Orders below $45 have a standard shipping fee added at checkout.

How will I know when my order has shipped?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with the carrier and tracking information so you can follow your treats on their way to your pup.

Can I use a coupon code?

Absolutely! If you have an active Barkery House coupon code, enter it during checkout and your eligible discount will be applied before you complete your purchase.
