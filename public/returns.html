RETURNS & ORDER ISSUES

Something Not Quite Right?

We want every Barkery House order to arrive ready for happy tails. If there's a problem with your order, we're here to help make things right.

Food Item Returns

Because our treats are food products, we cannot accept returns of opened or used treats. If you have a concern about your order, please contact us so we can review the situation with you.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or with an item missing, please let us know as soon as possible. Include your order number and details about the issue so we can determine the best way to help.

How To Get Help

Visit our Contact page to send us a message about your order. Be sure to include your order number whenever possible so we can locate your order and assist you more quickly.
