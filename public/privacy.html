YOUR PRIVACY

Your Information Matters.

We believe you should know what information is collected when you use The Barkery House website and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

When you use The Barkery House website, you may provide information such as your name, email address, shipping address, order details, and information submitted through our contact form.

We collect this information only when needed to process orders, provide customer support, or respond to messages you send through the website.

Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. The Barkery House does not directly collect or store your full credit or debit card information.

Stripe may process payment information necessary to complete your purchase in accordance with its own privacy practices.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to process and manage orders, arrange shipping, send order-related communications, respond to customer questions, and provide support.

We may also use order information to help maintain accurate records and ensure the website's shopping and checkout features work as intended.

Third-Party Services

The Barkery House uses trusted third-party services to help operate the website and process orders. These services include Stripe for payment processing, Supabase for order and database services, and Resend for transactional emails.

These providers may process information necessary to perform their services and maintain their own privacy practices.

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through The Barkery House website and limit its use to the purposes needed to operate the store, process orders, and provide customer support.

While no online system can guarantee complete security, we use established service providers and practices designed to help keep customer information protected.

Questions About Your Privacy?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled through The Barkery House website, please contact us.
